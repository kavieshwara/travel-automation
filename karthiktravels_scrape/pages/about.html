About Us

Karthik Travels was founded on 1985. With 30 years of successful business by making our customers happy and satisfied, we are marching ahead to explore new horizons by the goodwill and reputation that we have garnered so far in this service. The company is sphere-headed by our Managing Director Mr.R.Paarthasarathy (Vijayan), under his able guidance, we are striving to bring the best from us and to make our customer pleased and fulfilled.

We have a fleet of vehicles such as Buses, Vans, Tempo-Travelers, Call Taxis and many others. We are committed to provide Safe, Enjoyable, comfortable and Affordable travel to our customers. Our polite & skilled drivers, excellent maintenance support, and stringent adherence to On-time travel and high standards of safety measures - all contribute to our successful growth. We are well-known in the industry for our regularity and affordable service.

We are fully equipped with all kinds of latest vehicles like Indica, Innova, Qualis, Tempo traveler, Ambassador, Chevrolet Tavera, Swaraj Mazda, Omni, Coach etc.,.

We provide our service round the clock on all 365 days.
